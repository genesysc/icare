Frequently asked questions

The real fears, answered plainly.

I need sponsorship. Can you get it for me?

No. We cannot promise sponsorship, or find sponsored roles for you. Many employers cannot or will not sponsor. What we can do is make sure that when you do get a chance, you are ready for it. For visa or immigration questions, speak to a regulated adviser.

What if I just need any job to start?

That is completely fine. There is no shame in starting somewhere. We help you find realistic options and build from there, at your pace.

I've been rejected a lot. Will this really help?

Rejection is common, and it is rarely about you as a person. We look at what is not working, improve your CV and interviews, and keep you going. We cannot promise an outcome, but you will not be doing it alone.

Do you find jobs or apply for me?

No. You find and apply for roles. We follow up on applications you have already made, with your permission, and coach you through the process.
