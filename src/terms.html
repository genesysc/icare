Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone using iCare — candidates (carers, nurses, and other health and social care professionals building a profile) and employers (organisations searching for and hiring candidates). You must be 18 or older to create an account. By creating an account, you agree to these terms and our Privacy Policy.

Candidates: free, always

iCare never charges candidates for anything. No fee to sign up, build a profile, get found by employers, or use any feature of the platform. There is no premium profile, no paid boost, and no way to pay for better visibility — this isn't a pricing decision we might change, it's how the platform is built to work, in line with UK regulation governing recruitment services.

What we verify — and what we don't

Every claim on a candidate profile carries one of four trust grades, shown plainly wherever it appears:

  • Verified — checked directly against a public register or identity provider.
  • Evidenced — you uploaded a document and a person reviewed it.
  • Derived — computed from other information already on the platform.
  • Declared — you told us, and we haven't independently checked it.

On DBS checks specifically: we do not verify DBS certificates ourselves, and we never describe one as "verified" or "certified" by iCare. Where you've told us your certificate is on the DBS Update Service, we show that status honestly — but only an employer using the official Update Service can confirm it.

How employer search works

We're deliberate about what employers can see, and when, because we want hiring decisions made on merit. When a verified employer searches for candidates:

  • Search results show your name, current job title, and location, alongside your role, trust badges, skills, and experience summary.
  • Your photo, any introduction video, and your CV file are not shown until you've been shortlisted and you've explicitly consented to share them.
  • Your full contact details and DBS certificate number are held back further still, and only released after shortlisting, your consent, and — where a placement is actually being arranged — the reference and identity checks required by law.

Our search and any AI assistance we build into it works from the criteria an employer describes — it matches candidates against skills, experience, and logistics. It does not score, rank, or rate candidates as people, and it will not act on search criteria based on protected characteristics such as age, sex, race, religion, or disability.

Employer verification

We ask every employer to verify their organisation before they can search — for example via a CQC provider ID or Companies House number. We reserve the right to decline or revoke verification at our discretion.
