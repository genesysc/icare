    What a match tells you

    A match means the organisation was on the Home Office register on the date above, with an A-rated Skilled Worker licence. It does not confirm the licence is still in force today, that the employer has certificates of sponsorship available, or that it will sponsor a particular job.

    The register has no company numbers, so two employers can share a name, and job adverts often use a trading name rather than the legal one. Check the town against the job advert. Source: GOV.UK register of licensed sponsors.
