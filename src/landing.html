The problem

Right now, every move means starting over.

DBS and right-to-work checking in the UK is spread across dozens of separate, competing services, and every employer runs its own. Clear one set of checks and there's no way to carry any of it to the next job — the whole process simply begins again.

1,146 UK health-care staff recruitment agencies — a fragmented market, with no single platform covering the whole sector. IBISWorld, 2025
100,000+ NHS vacancies, with flexible staffing now described as a structural necessity rather than a short-term fix. Staffing Industry Analysts, 2025
£10.9bn Spent each year on temporary healthcare staffing — £5.1bn through agencies, plus £5.8bn on NHS bank staff. LaingBuisson · NHS England

What makes it trustworthy

A passport is only worth something if the stamps are honest.

So we grade every line of your profile, and show employers the grade alongside the claim. Honest beats impressive.

Verified

Checked by us directly at source.

Evidenced

You've uploaded a document that supports it.

Derived

Worked out from other things on your profile.

Declared

You've told us, and we've said so plainly.

We don't claim to verify DBS certificates. Your employer runs their own pre-employment checks, as they must, and an Update Service check stays theirs to run. What iCare does is put your status, your evidence and your consent in one place — and make sure nothing on your profile pretends to be more solid than it is.

Proven, just never this wide

The model already works.

NHS staff banks have run on exactly this idea for years: join once, get your registration, right to work and DBS checked once, then pick up work across more than 100 NHS Trusts and other NHS organisations without redoing any of it.

But that only covers NHS temporary and bank shifts. It stops at permanent roles, at private and independent care providers, at dental, at pharmacy. Step outside it and you're back to filling in the same forms again.

iCare takes the part that works — verify once, carry it with you — and opens it out across the whole sector: NHS and private, temporary and permanent, nursing through to dental, pharmacy and allied health.

What we will never do

  • ✕Score or rank you. No hidden rating decides who sees you. Employers get plain, explainable reasons for every match.
  • ✕Show your details before you say yes. Your name, photo and contact details stay hidden until you accept an invite.
  • ✕Keep the door open after. When a role closes or you withdraw, that employer's access to your profile ends.
  • ✕Share what you've kept private. You set what's public, what's for employers, and what nobody sees — and you can change it whenever.
