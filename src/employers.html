What is iCare?

iCare is a recruitment platform built specifically for health and social care — and it's the first in the UK to let you find candidates by just describing them. No filters, no checkboxes, no scrolling job boards. Tell our AI who you need, and it searches a growing pool of evidenced carers, nurses, and support workers to find real matches. Then run the whole hire — shortlisting, interviews, offers — right inside iCare. One platform, start to finish.

What sets us apart

How it works

From search to hired, without leaving the chat.

01

Ask, in plain English

Describe who you need — skills, certifications, experience, location. iCare's AI searches evidenced profiles and shows you real matches, headline info first: name, role, badges, location.

02

Shortlist with one click

See someone promising? Shortlist them straight from their card. No exporting, no separate tracker.

03

Ask "Who is…"

Get an AI-built summary of any shortlisted candidate — their experience, skills, training, and (where they've chosen to share it) who they are as a person, drawn from their own platform activity.

04

Interview without the repeat questions

Send your interview questions once. Candidates answer on video, in their own time. iCare's AI transcribes each response before it reaches you — so you review answers, not raw footage, and never ask the same question twice.

05

Progress and hire

Move candidates through offer and hire, or manage it all by chat: "send an offer to everyone successful in the last two weeks." Your call, your pace.
