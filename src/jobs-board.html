    How we check these jobs

    Licensed sponsor means the employer's name and town matched an A-rated Skilled Worker sponsor on the Home Office register on the date shown. We re-check every job against each day's register. A licence is not proof the employer will sponsor this particular job, or that it has certificates of sponsorship available. Ask the employer before you apply.

    Role meets visa rules means the advert gives an occupation code and a salary of at least £41,700 a year. Some health and care roles qualify on lower salaries; those jobs are listed without this badge rather than given a guess.

    UK-based switchers only: care worker and senior care worker roles closed to new overseas applicants on 22 July 2025. People already in the UK on a sponsored visa can still switch into them until 22 July 2028.

    General information, not immigration advice. Check gov.uk or a regulated adviser for your situation.
