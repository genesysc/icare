Who we are

iCare is built and operated by [Genesys Consultancy Ltd — company number and registered address to be confirmed]. For anything in this policy, contact us at [privacy contact email — to be set up once the icare.co.uk domain is live].

What we collect

What we collect depends on how you use iCare:

  • Waitlist sign-up: your name, email address, and (if you choose to give it) phone number. If you're signing up as an employer, also your organisation's name and, optionally, what roles you're typically hiring for.
  • Account creation: your email address, used to sign you in via a one-time code — we don't store passwords.
  • Candidate profiles: the information you choose to add — your professional headline, experience, skills, qualifications, employment history, availability, a profile photo if you upload one, and (if relevant) DBS and reference details, only with your explicit consent at each step.
  • Employer accounts: your organisation's details and the information you submit to verify your organisation (for example a CQC provider ID or Companies House number).
  • Usage data: basic technical information needed to run the service — timestamps, and session tokens stored in your browser so you stay signed in.

What we don't collect

When we build tools that read documents you upload (like a CV), we deliberately avoid extracting sensitive details — date of birth, nationality, immigration status, marital status, gender, religion, ethnicity, health information, or National Insurance numbers. If any of that turns up in something you upload, it gets flagged for your attention rather than stored as structured profile data.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and let you build and manage your profile.
  • To let verified employers search published candidate profiles and, if you consent, contact you about opportunities.
  • To send you service emails — confirming your account, letting you know when iCare launches, and (for waitlist sign-ups) an early-access welcome email.
  • To keep the platform secure and working as intended.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Candidate profile information is never shown in full to an employer without your consent — see our approach to shortlisting below. We use a small number of service providers to run iCare: [Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudflare (hosting), and — once set up — Sender.net (email delivery)]. These providers process data on our behalf under their own data processing agreements; we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Written shortlisting, before anything visual

When an employer searches for candidates, we're deliberate about what's shown and when, in order to keep hiring decisions fair. Full details of exactly what's visible at each stage are set out in our Terms of Service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we currently retain the data for [12 months — this default is provisional and pending legal confirmation] before permanent deletion, in case you want to reopen your account in that window. You can ask us to delete your data sooner — see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to any legal retention requirements).
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, contact us at [privacy contact email]. You can also close your account directly from your account settings once that feature is live.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits — this is essential to the service and isn't used for tracking or advertising. We don't currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as the product changes, and update the "last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be communicated by email where we have one on file for you.
